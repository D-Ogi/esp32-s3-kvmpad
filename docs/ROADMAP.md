# KVMpad roadmap

**Status dates** — November 2, 2026 for the base milestones and November 20, 2026 for the extension and media delivery plan in section 8. **Normative source for task contents:** `docs/spec/05-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md` (milestones
M0–M9, task backlog, order, Definition of Done) plus the ADRs in `docs/ADR/` for everything added after the specification was
frozen. This file adds what 05 cannot carry: the tasks introduced by ADRs, the current status of each item, the release gates as
they stand today, and the next concrete steps. No calendar dates: a single developer and hardware-gated milestones make dates
fiction; progress is measured by gates passed with evidence (`test/reports/`, `hardware/boards.md`).

Status values follow 05 §13: `DESIGNED` → `IMPLEMENTED_SW` → `READY_FOR_HIL` → `VERIFIED_HW` → `RELEASE_CANDIDATE` → `RELEASED`;
`BLOCKED` names an ADR or a failing test. `NEXT` marks the item being started.

## 8. Extension platform and media

The v1.3.1 intake adds HPE milestones M0–M8 and extension stages E0–E8. The workstreams below group acceptance tests by what can be demonstrated on unit 1. The release gates remain 1.3 Developer, 1.4 Connected and 1.5 Media Preview. The package's ticket and test IDs are mapped in [ADR-040](ADR/ADR-040-extension-platform.md).

**Current priority (November 20): USB-C monitor, epic 4.** The remaining acceptance work covers pointer motion and window dragging, desktop source resolution in interactive Windows Display settings, and reconnect, color and HID recovery across both modes. The table numbers identify workstreams. The proposed order after the monitor follows the three preview gates. Developer Preview covers local package installation and removal, declarative UI, local settings and an offline flight demo. Joystick HID requires a hardware pass. Connected Preview covers the HOST data broker, a live MSFS or X-Plane provider and instrument dashboards. Media Preview covers the consent-based Windows magnifier on Monitor 2. Paired package delivery, optional language modules, endurance and recovery remain in the acceptance table below. Player and Pilot Panel performance tuning remains paused at the owner's request.

Keyboard → 2-in-1 offers left and right touchpads beside the keys. Its top-edge touchpad takes over the top bar and offers a left- or right-hand drawer. Its handle accepts a tap or a horizontal swipe and gives 180 pixels back to the pad when closed. The smallest target in the US, German ISO and Japanese JIS number and function layers was 44 × 48 pixels. Physical finger tracking and two-finger scrolling in Windows still need a hands-on check. Player and extension performance tuning is paused at the measurements below.

Pilot Panel 1.3.0 runs six screens from one card archive. The board benchmark opens radio, Setup and the aircraft checklists. Normal touch installation of this revision still needs confirmation. Reading the live chart camera confirmed 600% zoom. Moving the chart by four pixels took about 122 ms to present. Switching back to the radio took 305 ms after adopting native-size numerals, down from a 376 ms mean. [Physical measurements](../test/reports/2026-11-19_flight-host-telemetry_022168e4.yaml)

When work resumes, the Pilot Panel acceptance steps are (1) confirm chart responsiveness by touch, (2) use the existing paired HOST for COM and transponder readback and commands with a running simulator, then qualify autopilot modes, and (3) save aircraft selection, checklist work and ATC notes across launches. The moving VFR map, MCDU and ILS follow once the simulator connection is proven. The existing airport charts support offline study. Displaying aircraft position and track needs the simulator link and georeferenced map data.

Media playback and capture remain a separate open release workstream. Unit 1 played twelve prepared MP4 samples at Fit and 1:1, covering three source sizes and four frame rates. At a 25 fps source rate, 320×180, 640×360 and 960×540 reached 16.37, 12.88 and 6.36 fps at 1:1. Fit reached 4.68, 4.40 and 3.55 fps. All 966 pictures were presented, and the panel completed all 7213 refreshes. Source-rate playback at Fit and sustained capture from Monitor 2 remain open. [Codec and display comparisons](../test/reports/2026-11-19_device-media_022168e4.yaml)
